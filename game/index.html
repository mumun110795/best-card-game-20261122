EMBERFALL

Duel of the Four Sparks — a card duel against the Ashen Warden

Burn the Warden from 22 life to 0 before your own 20 fade.

Each turn your ember grows and refills. Play cards to strike, guard, mend and scheme. Play two cards of a suit in a row and the second is Kindled — stronger.

The Warden telegraphs its next play in the red banner. Read it, and answer it: raise Guard against a Strike, out-race a Ward, leech back a Leech.
